**[*Funny Bone Favorites*](https://www.discogs.com/master/342333-Various-Funny-Bone-Favorites){:target="_blank"}** was an 8-track we had when we owned the Cadillac.  As I discussed in **[*Readers Digest's Golden Age Of Entertainment*](2021-10-18-golden_age_of_entertainment.html)** we had a car with an 8-track player and like that title, this was one of the titles we had on 8-track and would listen to when we went on family vacations.

Today is my brother's birthday and although we don't talk any more and I'm not even sure where he is currently living, I think of him when I listen to this record or my [Spotify playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4n74I8SxY33bOMSTzW8kTg?si=f3a89bb8a42a4853){:target="_blank"} of this album (which is what I'm listening to right now).  This was a [Ronco](https://www.discogs.com/label/15604-Ronco){:target="_blank"} product that my mother may have ordered for us from an ad on TV.  As much as my brother and I enjoyed it, I think she may have enjoyed it also.  When I found the LP a few years ago I remember thinking that this could have easily been a Doctor Demento compilation.

**Star Trek: The Motion Picture**, **Star Trek II: The Wrath of Kahn**, **Star Trek III: The Search For Spock**, **Star Trek IV: The Voyage Home** returned to theaters on September 4, 5, 6, and 7.  Each movie was paired with an episode from the original series.  "The Changeling," "Space Seed," "Amok Time," and "The City on the Edge of Forever."

I went all 4 nights.  

The most interesting thing about seeing these in the theater was seeing the episodes of the original series on the big screen.  These episodes were not made for a wide-screen theatrical presentation.  It's a lot easier on the big screen to see the sets and backgrounds as sets and backgrounds.  The sound was also, obviously, not made for a movie theater.  That being said, it was still great to see these in the theater and the pairing of the episodes with the movies was excellent.

This also marked the first time in about 40 years that I've seen **Star Trek III: The Search For Spock** in a movie theater.  It is usually considered a lesser Star Trek movie, but seeing it again, it's still a great movie.

On the 4th night, Faye came with me to see **Star Trek IV: The Voyage Home**.  It is easily the Star Trek movie with the most popular appeal.

Star Trek fans often describe **Star Trek IV** as "The one with the whales.  I always think of them in this way:

- "Where nomad has gone before"
- "The one where Spock dies"
- "The one where Spock comes back to life"
- "The one with the whales"
- "Shatner's Folly"
- "The last one"

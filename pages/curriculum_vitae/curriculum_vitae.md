## Employment

### Northeast Kansas Library System 

- 2016-present
- Next Search Catalog Coordinator
- I manage the shared catalog for the Next Search Catalog (previously known as "NExpress") library consortium in northeast Kansas.  Next Search Catalog uses the Koha open source ILS and Aspen Library Software as its discovery layer.  Currently there are 44 member libraries operating 50 branch locations in the 14 counties of north eastern Kansas.

### Latah County Library District 

- 2006-2016
- Access Services Manager 
- As Access Services Manager for LCLD, I was primarily responsible for all of the circulation functions at all 7 branches of LCLD - overseeing 8 regular employees at the Moscow Branch's circulation desk, 2 employees in branch services, helping supervise 6 branch managers outside of the Moscow branch, and scheduling, training, and supervising approximately 10-15 substitute employees.  I oversaw our materials rotation process as it relates to branch services as well as our circulation interface with the rest of the 49 member VALNet library consortium.  I acted as the interim webmaster for LCLD.  Additionally, I was the system administrator for VALNet's Koha ILS and the key point of contact between the VALNet consortium and ByWater Solutions, VALNet's Koha support partner.

### University of Idaho 

- 2002-2006
- Graduate Teaching Assistant / Lecturer
- I taught classes while attending graduate school and for several semesters afterwards as a Lecturer at U of I.

### Boise Public Library 

- 1997-2002
- Bookmobile Specialist
- I was the person primarily responsible for running all of the day to day activities on the library's Bookmobile.  Since the Bookmobile was part of the Homebound/Bookmobile Services department, I also helped from time to time with deliveries of materials to homebound patrons.

### University of Idaho Library 

- 1996-1997
- Student Employee
- I worked as a student circulation desk employee while taking classes as an undergraduate at the University of Idaho.

### WolfCon25

  - PRESENTATION: Ad Astra per Aspera: Koha use and community in Kansas
  - With Jason Robb from Southeast Kansas Library System
  - [Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1lXCrnLuSv_z35IgDfBn-r_WwMEMlu5yPep8m-rysPIQ/edit?usp=sharing)

Two additional consortia have migrated to Koha within the last few years.  We will talk about our own system's history with Koha, why we chose Koha, and how we work together in Kansas, with the Koha community in the USA, and with the Koha community world-wide.  As the Koha community works towards joining the OLF, we hope to introduce Koha community to the attendees of WOLFcon2025.
